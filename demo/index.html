Hidden Demand Explorer

Estimating true fresh-produce demand in dark stores during stockouts · Team ASCI · IR&P IIT Gandhinagar × Eternal
Real, anonymised data from FreshRetailNet-50K (898 fresh-grocery stores, China, 2024). Store, city and product are encoded IDs; sales are in normalised units.

What this prototype shows

When an item sells out, recorded sales stop counting demand. Forecasts built on those sales order less next time, and the shelf empties earlier.

  • Explorer: one item, one day: what was recorded, when it sold out, and the demand we estimate was lost.
  • Orders: tomorrow's order from recorded sales vs from recovered demand, with the reason.
  • Network: which store-items hide the most demand across all 50,000 series.
  • Evaluation: in a simulated store where true demand is known, how orders compare.
