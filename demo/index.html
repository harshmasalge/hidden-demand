Hidden Demand Explorer

Pick a store and item to see what sales recorded, when the shelf went empty, and the demand we estimate was lost, then compare tomorrow’s order both ways.

Real dataFreshRetailNet-50K: 898 fresh-grocery stores, 2024. Store, city and product are encoded IDs; sales are in normalised units.

What you can do here

When an item sells out, recorded sales stop counting demand. Forecasts built on those sales order less next time, and the shelf empties earlier.

  • What was recorded, when it sold out, and the demand we estimate was lost.

  • Tomorrow’s order from recorded sales vs from recovered demand, with the reason.

  • Which store-items hide the most demand across all 50,000 series.

  • In a simulated store where true demand is known, how orders compare.
